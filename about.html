About Bethel Fashion

Bethel Fashion is a fashion retailer situated in Johannesburg, South Africa. The business is associated with women's clothing, shoes, and fashion accessories. Bethel Fashion also uses social media to communicate with customers and share information about new stock, products, orders, and prices.

Our Story

Bethel Fashion is a Johannesburg based fashion retailer that provides women's clothing, shoes, and fashion accessories. The business provides customers with access to a range of fashion products and communicates with customers through social media.

Bethel Fashion is located at 180 Rahima Moosa Street, Johannesburg, South Africa. The business uses social media to promote its products, communicate with customers, and provide information about available stock, orders, and prices.

What We Provide

Bethel Fashion offers women's apparel, shoes, and fashion accessories. These products provide customers with different fashion options for their clothing and personal style needs.
